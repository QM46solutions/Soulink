The problem

Young Indians spend hours on social apps, but making a real connection on them is hard.

Built for followers

Global apps reward reach and likes. Finding people you'd actually get along with is left to luck.

Low trust

Fake profiles and unwanted DMs make people, especially women, wary of meeting anyone new.

Not made here

Little is designed around Indian languages, college life, city events and the way friendships form here.

Three pains: apps optimise for audience not friendship; trust is low because anyone can message anyone with any face; and the products aren't built for India's languages and campus/city culture.
