Nikhil Cheedella, Founder · Sai Raghava Ragishetty, Co-founder · QM46 Solutions Pvt Ltd

Open with the one-line idea: a social app built in India, for Indian youth, around real people and real compatibility rather than follower counts. We're raising ₹1.5 crore pre-seed.

The problem

Young Indians spend hours on social apps, but making a real connection on them is hard.

Built for followers

Global apps reward reach and likes. Finding people you'd actually get along with is left to luck.

Low trust

Fake profiles and unwanted DMs make people, especially women, wary of meeting anyone new.

Not made here

Little is designed around Indian languages, college life, city events and the way friendships form here.

Three pains: apps optimise for audience not friendship; trust is low because anyone can message anyone with any face; and the products aren't built for India's languages and campus/city culture.

The solution

Soulink connects real people by compatibility, and only when both say yes.

Face-verified

Every account passes a live face scan, and one face can't open a second account.

Soul DNA

A short quiz on values, nature and thinking gives every pair of people a Soul Score.

Connect with intent

Requests say what you're hoping for. Chat opens only after the other person accepts.

The three pillars: verified faces for trust, Soul DNA for compatibility, and consent-first connecting (stated intent, accept / reject / hold, no messaging strangers).
