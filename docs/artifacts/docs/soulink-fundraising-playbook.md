# Soulink Fundraising Playbook

Oct 14, 2026 · @SaiRaghava

## The round

Raise **₹1.5 crore pre-seed for about 12.5%** (₹10.5 crore pre-money, an opening position), enough for 24 months in the financial model. Run it in about 10 weeks, starting with a ₹20 lakh government grant that takes no equity.

What you send every investor:

- **Pitch deck** (Soulink Pitch Deck, 12 slides) — fill in the \[brackets\]: pilot numbers, Nikhil's role, contact.
- **Financial model** (Soulink\_Financial\_Model.xlsx) — for anyone who asks after the first call.
- **A 2-minute demo video** of the app on a phone: sign up, Soul DNA, Connect, a Spark, Argue.

## Before you pitch

The company is already registered as QM46 Solutions, so investors have an entity to put money into. These come next, and the pilot numbers matter most.

- [ ] **Sign a founders' agreement for QM46 Solutions: equity split, roles, vesting over 4 years. Check the company type: investors usually expect a Private Limited company**.
- [ ] Check your employment contract with your current employer for side-business and IP clauses before you pitch publicly.
- [ ] Apply for **DPIIT startup recognition** on startupindia.gov.in (free; needed for the Seed Fund grant and tax benefits).
- [ ] File the **Soulink trademark** (class 9 and 45).
- [ ] Run a **pilot**: 300–500 students at 2–3 Hyderabad colleges for 4 weeks. Track sign-ups, weekly active users, and how many come back in week 4.
- [ ] Set up **Razorpay** so Premium, Spread around and paid messages take real money.
- [ ] Record the demo video and put the deck's numbers in the \[brackets\].

## Outreach plan

Ten weeks from setup to a signed term sheet. Talk to many investors in the same few weeks, so offers arrive close together.

1. **Weeks 1–2 — Set up.** Company, founders' agreement, DPIIT, trademark. Start the college pilot.
2. **Weeks 3–4 — Prove it.** Run the pilot, collect numbers and 5–10 short user quotes. Fill the deck's brackets and record the demo.
3. **Week 4 — Grant.** Apply for the Startup India Seed Fund through an empanelled incubator in Hyderabad.
4. **Weeks 5–6 — Warm intros.** List 40 names from the table. For each, find one person who knows them (LinkedIn mutuals, TiE, college alumni) and ask for a short intro using the forwardable email below.
5. **Weeks 5–8 — Meetings.** Aim for 25 first calls. Show the app live within the first 5 minutes. Send the model only to people who ask.
6. **Weeks 7–9 — Lead investor.** One investor sets the terms (amount, valuation, iSAFE or equity); others follow. Ask every warm investor "what would you need to see to lead?"
7. **Weeks 9–10 — Close.** Have a startup lawyer check the term sheet. Collect the money, issue shares, and send investors a monthly update from then on.

Keep a simple tracker: investor, who introduced you, date of each call, next step, and their answer.

## Questions investors will ask

| Question | Short answer |
| --- | --- |
| Why won't Instagram just copy this? | Instagram is built for reach. Copying verified faces, consent-first chat and Soul DNA would cut against its own growth. Our verified network and compatibility data grow with every user. |
| Koo shut down. Why is this different? | Koo spent most of one round on paid promotion and earned almost nothing. We grow through campuses and events, keep marketing at ₹1.5 lakh a month, and earn from ads and brand deals from month 4. |
| Are you full-time? | Say plainly when each founder goes full-time. Most investors want at least one full-time founder at or before the money arrives. |
| How do you keep it safe? | Face verification on every account, no messages without accepted connections, comment reports that hide abuse, and a block list. |
| What do you need to show for the next round? | About a lakh monthly active users in Hyderabad, steady week-4 retention, and repeat brand partners. |
| What happens to face data? | Explain where it's stored, that it's only used to verify and stop duplicate accounts, and how users delete it. India's DPDP Act will matter here. |
