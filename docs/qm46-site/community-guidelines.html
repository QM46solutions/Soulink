Community Guidelines

QM46 Solutions Private Limited · Last updated 13 November 2026

Soulink is for real people making real connections. These rules keep it that way.

Be real

One person, one account. No fake profiles, no impersonation, no AI faces of real people without consent.

Be respectful

No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech, or insults about religion, caste, gender, sexuality, region or disability. Respect a "no": stop messaging people who aren't interested.

Keep it safe

No nudity or sexual content, nothing involving minors, no violence, self-harm encouragement, drugs, weapons or scams. Never share someone's private photos, phone number or address.

Keep it honest

No false news presented as fact, no fake engagement, no spam. Mark paid partnerships as ads.

Respect creators

Post your own work. Use music from Soulink's music library, your video's own sound, or songs you have the rights to. Copyright owners can report misuse, and 3 copyright strikes in 12 months remove an account.

What happens if rules are broken

Tap ❗ to mark something offensive. Content many people mark is hidden (comments) or suspended (posts and Sparks). We remove illegal content, limit or close repeat offenders, and report serious crimes to the police.
