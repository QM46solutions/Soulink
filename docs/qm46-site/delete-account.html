Delete your Soulink account

QM46 Solutions Private Limited · Last updated 13 November 2026

You can delete your Soulink account (by QM46 Solutions Private Limited) at any time, from inside the app.

How to delete

Open Soulink and log in.

Go to Profile › Settings › Delete my account.

Type DELETE and confirm. Your account is hidden at once and you are logged out everywhere.

What happens next

A 7-day cooling period starts, to give you time to change your mind and us time to check everything. Log in before then and the deletion is cancelled.

After 7 days your profile, photos, Soul DNA, posts, Sparks and chats are erased for good.

For 6 months after that, the same face, phone number or email cannot be used to create a new Soulink account. Your face fingerprint, phone number and email are kept encrypted, used only for this check, and then erased.

Some account records are kept for 1 year where Indian law requires it (see the Privacy Policy), and then deleted.

Delete some of your data and keep your account

Posts, Sparks, Moments and comments: open it, tap ⋯ (or the bin icon) and choose Delete. It is erased from Soulink.

Chat messages: press and hold a message you sent and tap Unsend. It is removed for everyone in the chat.

Location: Settings › Location › Stop using my location. Your saved location is deleted.

Activity used for matching: Settings › App permissions › switch off "Use my activity for Soul DNA". Soulink stops using it, and what was kept is deleted within 90 days.

Profile details and photo: Edit profile, then remove or change them.

Anything else: email us from the phone number or email on your account, say what you want deleted, and we will do it within 7 days.

Can't open the app?

Email quantum46solutions@gmail.com from the email or phone number linked to your account and ask us to delete it.
