Where our story begins

Socializing was never
new to India.

Long before apps existed, villages met under the banyan tree: at the chaupal, the chai stall, the temple steps. That was a social network too.

Everyone knew who they were talking to. There were no anonymous strangers.

There were clear limits. People knew what to say, what not to say, and how to behave.

Elders guided us. They told us what was right and taught us to respect each other's space.
